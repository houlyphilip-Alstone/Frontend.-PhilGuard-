ABOUT PHILGUARD

Security infrastructure for sensitive email.

PhilGuard is designed around a simple idea: security controls should be powerful enough for serious engineering teams while remaining understandable to the people who use them every day.

Our mission

Help organisations protect sensitive email information with encryption, controlled access, auditability and developer-friendly APIs.

Built for Kenya, designed to scale

PhilGuard is being developed with Kenyan organisations in mind while keeping the architecture suitable for international growth.
